Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Who we are

The Sunday Table is operated by Radhe foods LLC. Postal correspondence may be sent to 1622 SW Bayshore Blvd, Port St. Lucie, FL 34984, United States.

Information we collect

You can read every recipe without creating an account or giving us personal information. Information may be collected in these ways:

  • Postal correspondence, including any contact details and message you choose to include in a letter sent to our mailing address.
  • Technical information that the hosting service may process to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of request.
  • Information stored on your device, described under "Cookies and local storage".

Postal correspondence

If you write to us at the postal address on our contact page, your letter may contain personal information. We use information you include to handle your inquiry. Do not send sensitive personal information by post.

Newsletter subscriptions

The Sunday Table does not currently offer a newsletter.

Cookies and local storage

The site itself does not set cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember recipes you save with the "Save" button. Saved recipe names stay on your device and can be removed by clearing this site's browser storage.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads.

Analytics

We use Umami Cloud to measure visits to this site. Its tracker loads automatically on site pages and may record page views and related visit information such as referrers, device and browser details, and location estimates. The script is configured to exclude URL search parameters. Umami states that its tracker works without cookies or cross-site tracking and does not automatically collect personal data. See the Umami documentation. Analytics use is subject to the privacy rules that apply in the regions where this site is used.

Advertising

This site does not currently display third-party advertisements or use advertising, conversion-tracking or remarketing tags.

Third-party services

  • Unsplash serves food photographs, so your browser requests images from Unsplash's servers.
  • Google Fonts serves typefaces, as described above.
  • Umami Cloud provides automatically loaded site analytics; see the Umami documentation.
  • Hosting provider serves the site and may process technical logs under its own terms.

Data retention

We keep postal correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry and meet record-keeping obligations. Hosting-log and Umami Cloud analytics retention follow the providers' current service settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear saved recipes at any time by clearing your browser's storage for this site.
  • Block or delete cookies using your browser settings.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by writing to the postal address on our contact page.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

External links

Pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, write to us at the postal address on our contact page.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, for example if advertising or a newsletter is added, and revise the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to the postal address on our contact page.
