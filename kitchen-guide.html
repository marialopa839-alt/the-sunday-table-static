Measuring

Our recipes give US cup measures alongside grams for baking ingredients. For cakes, cheesecake and pizza dough, a digital scale is the most reliable option. If you use cups, spoon flour into the cup and level it with a knife rather than scooping, which packs in extra flour.

Heat and pans

Medium-high heat is for searing and browning; medium for sautéing onions and garlic; medium-low for eggs, pancakes and gentle sauces. A pan is ready for searing when a drop of water skitters across the surface. Heavy pans, such as cast iron or thick stainless steel, hold heat better and brown more evenly than thin ones.

Seasoning

Recipes here use fine table salt in measured amounts unless "flaky salt" is specified. If you use coarse kosher salt, you may need a little more by volume. Taste at the end of cooking and adjust. Acid, such as lemon juice or vinegar, often fixes a dish that tastes flat when salt alone does not.

Safe internal temperatures

An instant-read thermometer is the best tool for doneness. Widely used food-safety guidance in the US recommends:

Recommended minimum internal temperatures
FoodMinimum internal temperature
Poultry (whole or pieces, ground)165°F / 74°C
Ground beef, pork160°F / 71°C
Fish145°F / 63°C (many cooks prefer salmon slightly lower; see recipe notes)
Egg dishes160°F / 71°C
Reheated leftovers165°F / 74°C

Guidance varies by country; check your local food-safety authority.

Storing leftovers

  • Refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours of cooking (1 hour in hot weather).
  • Shallow containers cool food faster and more safely than deep ones.
  • Most cooked dishes keep 3 to 4 days in the refrigerator. Each recipe gives specific guidance.
  • Label frozen food with the date. Thaw in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

A useful pantry

With these on hand, most recipes on this site need only a short shopping trip for fresh ingredients:

  • Olive oil and a neutral oil
  • Fine salt and flaky salt
  • Black peppercorns, red pepper flakes
  • Ground cumin, smoked paprika, chili powder, dried oregano
  • Canned tomatoes and tomato paste
  • Canned chickpeas and black beans
  • Dried pasta, rice, quinoa, rolled oats
  • Soy sauce, honey, Dijon mustard
  • Red wine vinegar and rice vinegar
  • Garlic, onions and lemons
