Homemade Margherita Pizza

A simple same-day dough, raw tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella and basil, baked as hot as your oven goes.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

Margherita is the pizza that leaves nowhere to hide: dough, tomato, cheese, basil. Because it is so plain, each element needs care.

Home ovens cannot reach pizzeria temperatures, so this method heats a baking steel or upside-down sheet pan for a full 45 minutes and finishes under the broiler for colour on top.

Method

  1. Mix flour, salt and yeast. Add water and oil and stir into a shaggy dough. Knead 8 to 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.

  2. Place in an oiled bowl, cover and let rise at room temperature 1 1/2 to 2 hours until doubled.

  3. Place a baking steel, stone or inverted heavy sheet pan on a rack in the upper third of the oven and heat to 500 to 550°F (260 to 290°C) for 45 minutes.

  4. Divide the dough in two and shape into balls. Rest 15 minutes, then stretch each into a 12-inch round on a floured surface, leaving the edge thicker.

  5. Season the tomatoes with salt. Spread a thin layer over the dough and scatter mozzarella over it.

  6. Slide onto the hot surface and bake 7 to 9 minutes. Switch to broil for the final 1 to 2 minutes if the top needs more colour. Finish with basil and olive oil.

Cook's notes

  • Use less sauce than you think. Too much makes the centre soggy.
  • Drain fresh mozzarella well for the same reason.
  • Use a well-floured overturned baking sheet if you do not have a pizza peel.

Substitutions

  • All-purpose flour works; the crust will be slightly softer.
  • Low-moisture mozzarella gives a more classic American melt.
  • Use store-bought dough on busy nights.

Variations

  • Add thin slices of garlic under the cheese.
  • Finish with a few slices of prosciutto after baking.
  • Cold-ferment the dough in the refrigerator for 24 to 72 hours for more flavour.

Storage

Refrigerate leftover slices up to 3 days. Dough balls can be frozen up to 2 months; thaw overnight in the refrigerator.

Reheating

Reheat slices in a covered skillet over medium heat 4 to 5 minutes to re-crisp the base.

Questions

Why is my pizza crust pale?

The oven or baking surface was not hot enough. Preheat longer and position the steel near the top of the oven.
