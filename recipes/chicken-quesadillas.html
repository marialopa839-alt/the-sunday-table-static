Chicken Quesadillas

Crisp, golden tortillas filled with shredded chicken, melty cheese and peppers.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

Quesadillas are the most practical use for leftover or rotisserie chicken. Seasoned lightly and paired with two kinds of cheese, they turn into a satisfying lunch in about fifteen minutes.

A medium flame is the trick: hot enough to crisp the tortilla, gentle enough to melt the cheese all the way through before the outside burns.

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook pepper and onion 5 to 6 minutes until soft. Transfer to a bowl.

  2. Toss the chicken with cumin, chili powder and salt.

  3. Lay out tortillas. Over half of each, scatter cheese, chicken and vegetables, then a little more cheese on top. Fold closed.

  4. Wipe the skillet, brush lightly with oil and heat over medium. Cook quesadillas 2 to 3 minutes per side, pressing gently, until golden and the cheese has melted.

  5. Rest 1 minute, cut into wedges and serve with salsa and sour cream.

Cook's notes

  • Cheese on both sides of the filling acts as glue.
  • Keep finished quesadillas on a rack in a low oven so they stay crisp.
  • Grate your own cheese; pre-shredded cheese melts less smoothly.

Substitutions

  • Use black beans for a vegetarian version.
  • Pepper Jack adds heat.
  • Corn tortillas make smaller, crisper quesadillas.

Variations

  • Add sautéed mushrooms and spinach.
  • Stir chipotle in adobo into the chicken.
  • Bake several at once on a sheet pan at 425°F (220°C) for 10 minutes, flipping halfway.

Storage

Refrigerate up to 3 days, wrapped.

Reheating

Re-crisp in a dry skillet over medium-low heat 2 to 3 minutes per side, or in a 375°F (190°C) oven for 8 minutes.
