Classic Beef Tacos

Home-blended taco seasoning, browned beef and a quick tomato salsa for an easy family dinner.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

Ground beef tacos are a weeknight standard for a reason. Mixing your own seasoning takes a minute and lets you control the salt and heat.

The key step is letting the beef actually brown before the seasoning goes in. Grey, steamed meat tastes flat; well-browned meat tastes rich.

Method

  1. Stir the salsa ingredients together and set aside to let the flavours meet.

  2. Mix the seasoning ingredients in a small bowl.

  3. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the beef and press it into a flat layer. Cook undisturbed 4 minutes until browned underneath, then break it up.

  4. Add the onion and cook 3 minutes more. Spoon off excess fat if needed.

  5. Stir in the seasoning and tomato paste for 1 minute, then add the water and simmer 3 to 4 minutes until saucy but not wet.

  6. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or directly over a gas flame for a few seconds per side. Fill and top as you like.

Cook's notes

  • Warm tortillas are pliable; cold ones crack.
  • Stack warmed tortillas in a clean towel to keep them soft.
  • Salt the salsa just before serving so it does not get watery.

Substitutions

  • Ground turkey works; add 1 tablespoon oil since it is leaner.
  • Use black beans or lentils for a vegetarian filling.
  • Swap the jalapeño for a pinch of chili flakes.

Variations

  • Crisp the tortillas lightly in oil for a crunchy shell.
  • Add pickled red onions and avocado.
  • Turn leftovers into a rice bowl.

Storage

Refrigerate beef up to 4 days; salsa is best within 2 days. Freeze cooked beef up to 3 months.

Reheating

Warm beef in a skillet with a splash of water over medium heat until hot throughout.

Questions

Corn or flour tortillas?

Either. Corn tortillas have more flavour and are naturally gluten-free; flour tortillas are softer and hold up to heavier fillings.
