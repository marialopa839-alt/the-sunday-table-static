Grilled Salmon Salad

Flaky grilled salmon over greens with cucumber, avocado and a mustard-dill vinaigrette.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

This salad leans on simple contrasts: warm fish on cool greens, creamy avocado against crunchy cucumber, and a vinaigrette with enough mustard to stand up to the salmon.

If you do not have a grill, a grill pan or a very hot skillet works just as well. The goal is a lightly charred exterior and a just-cooked centre.

Method

  1. Shake the vinaigrette ingredients together in a jar.

  2. Heat a grill or grill pan to medium-high and oil the grates.

  3. Rub the salmon with oil, salt and lemon zest.

  4. Grill skin side down 4 to 5 minutes, flip carefully and cook 2 to 3 minutes more until it flakes but is still slightly translucent in the centre.

  5. Toss greens, cucumber, tomatoes and onion with half the vinaigrette.

  6. Divide among plates, top with salmon and avocado, and spoon the remaining vinaigrette over the fish.

Cook's notes

  • Clean, hot, oiled grates are what keep salmon from sticking.
  • Leave the skin on during grilling; it protects the flesh.
  • Dress greens just before serving.

Substitutions

  • Use trout or arctic char.
  • Swap dill for parsley or chives.
  • Lemon juice instead of vinegar.

Variations

  • Add cooked farro or quinoa for a more filling plate.
  • Add sliced radishes and capers.
  • Use leftover honey garlic salmon, served cold.

Storage

Store salmon and salad separately, refrigerated, up to 2 days.

Reheating

Best served at room temperature or cold rather than reheated.

Questions

Can I bake the salmon instead?

Yes. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 10 to 12 minutes depending on thickness.
