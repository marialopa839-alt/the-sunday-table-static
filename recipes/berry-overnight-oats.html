Berry Overnight Oats

A make-ahead jar of creamy oats, yogurt and berries that is ready when you wake up.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

Overnight oats are less a recipe than a ratio: equal parts oats and liquid, plus a spoonful of yogurt for body. Once that is right, the rest is personal taste.

Chia seeds thicken the mixture and keep it from turning soupy as the berries release juice. Make two or three jars on a Sunday and breakfast is handled for half the week.

Method

  1. In a bowl or large jar, stir together the oats, milk, yogurt, chia seeds, maple syrup, vanilla and salt.

  2. Divide between two jars or containers. Top each with half of the berries.

  3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours or overnight.

  4. In the morning, stir, loosen with a splash of milk if it is too thick, and add the toasted nuts just before eating.

Cook's notes

  • Use rolled oats, not quick oats (which turn mushy) or steel-cut (which stay too firm without cooking).
  • Frozen berries work well; they thaw overnight and tint the oats.
  • Add crunchy toppings at the last moment so they stay crisp.

Substitutions

  • Dairy-free: use oat or almond milk and a coconut-based yogurt.
  • Gluten-free: use oats labelled gluten-free.
  • Swap chia seeds for ground flaxseed.

Variations

  • Peanut butter and banana: stir in 1 tablespoon peanut butter and top with sliced banana.
  • Apple pie: grated apple, cinnamon and a pinch of nutmeg.
  • Cocoa: add 1 tablespoon cocoa powder and a little extra sweetener.

Storage

Keeps in the refrigerator in sealed jars for up to 4 days. Add fresh berries and nuts on the day you eat it for the best texture.

Reheating

Usually eaten cold. To warm, microwave in a heatproof bowl for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring halfway, with a splash of milk.

Questions

Do I have to cook the oats?

No. Soaking softens rolled oats enough to eat without cooking.

Why is mine too thick?

Oats and chia keep absorbing liquid. Stir in a few tablespoons of milk before serving.
