One-Pan Lemon Chicken

Chicken thighs roasted with potatoes, lemon and oregano on a single sheet pan.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

Everything for this dinner cooks together on one pan, and the potatoes absorb the chicken juices and lemon as they roast.

Starting the potatoes ten minutes before the chicken means everything finishes at the same moment, with crisp skin and tender, golden potatoes.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss potatoes and onion with 1 tablespoon oil and 1/2 teaspoon salt on a large sheet pan. Roast 10 minutes.

  2. Meanwhile, mix the remaining oil, oregano, garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, pepper and remaining salt. Rub all over the chicken.

  3. Push the potatoes to the edges and set the chicken skin side up in the centre. Tuck in lemon slices.

  4. Roast 30 to 35 minutes, until the skin is crisp and the chicken reaches 175°F (80°C).

  5. Move the chicken to a board. Toss spinach with the hot potatoes and pan juices until it wilts.

  6. Serve with parsley scattered over.

Cook's notes

  • Dry chicken skin very well for the best crisp.
  • Use a large pan with space between pieces.
  • If the skin needs more colour, broil for 2 minutes at the end.

Substitutions

  • Boneless thighs: reduce roasting time to about 20 minutes.
  • Fresh thyme or rosemary instead of oregano.
  • Cubed sweet potatoes instead of baby potatoes.

Variations

  • Add olives and cherry tomatoes for the final 15 minutes.
  • Finish with crumbled feta.
  • Add a can of drained chickpeas with the chicken.

Storage

Refrigerate up to 4 days.

Reheating

Reheat at 375°F (190°C) for 12 to 15 minutes to re-crisp the skin.

Questions

Can I use chicken breasts?

Yes, bone-in breasts work but take 35 to 40 minutes and should be cooked to 165°F (74°C).
