Chicken & Vegetable Stir Fry

Tender chicken, crisp vegetables and a glossy ginger-garlic sauce, cooked fast over high heat.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

Stir-frying is quick once you are prepared, and almost impossible to manage if you are chopping as you go. Have every ingredient cut and the sauce mixed before you turn on the heat.

Tossing the chicken in a little cornstarch, a technique similar to velveting, keeps it tender and helps the sauce cling.

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with soy sauce and cornstarch and let sit 10 minutes. Whisk the sauce ingredients together.

  2. Heat a wok or large skillet over high heat until just smoking. Add 1 tablespoon oil and the chicken in one layer. Cook 2 minutes undisturbed, then stir-fry 2 minutes more until cooked through. Remove to a plate.

  3. Add remaining oil, then the broccoli and carrot. Stir-fry 2 minutes. Add a splash of water and cover for 1 minute to steam.

  4. Add the pepper and snap peas and stir-fry 1 to 2 minutes until crisp-tender.

  5. Push the vegetables aside, add garlic and ginger to the cleared space and stir 30 seconds.

  6. Return the chicken, re-whisk the sauce and pour it in. Toss 1 minute until glossy. Add green onions and serve over rice.

Cook's notes

  • Slice chicken while partly frozen for thin, even pieces.
  • Cook in batches if your pan is small; crowding causes steaming.
  • Always re-whisk the sauce because cornstarch settles.

Substitutions

  • Tamari and gluten-free oyster sauce for a gluten-free version.
  • Use tofu, pressed and cubed, instead of chicken.
  • Any firm vegetables work: bok choy, mushrooms, zucchini.

Variations

  • Add chili garlic sauce for heat.
  • Finish with toasted cashews.
  • Serve over noodles instead of rice.

Storage

Refrigerate up to 3 days.

Reheating

Reheat in a hot skillet for 2 to 3 minutes with a splash of water, or microwave covered.

Questions

Do I need a wok?

No. A large, heavy skillet works. The key is high heat and not overcrowding.
