Creamy Tuscan Pasta

Sun-dried tomatoes, spinach and garlic in a parmesan cream sauce with plenty of black pepper.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

The name is more American restaurant than Tuscan tradition, but the combination has earned its place: sweet-tart sun-dried tomatoes cut through a rich sauce, and spinach adds colour and freshness.

Using a little of the oil from the sun-dried tomato jar to cook the garlic is an easy way to layer in flavour without any extra ingredients.

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in well-salted boiling water until just al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water and drain.

  2. In a large skillet, warm the sun-dried tomato oil over medium heat. Add garlic and dried herbs and cook 1 minute.

  3. Add the sun-dried tomatoes and cook 1 minute more.

  4. Pour in cream and stock and simmer gently 4 to 5 minutes until slightly thickened.

  5. Stir in spinach a handful at a time until wilted. Lower heat and stir in parmesan.

  6. Add the pasta and toss, loosening with pasta water. Season with pepper and salt to taste, and serve with lemon.

Cook's notes

  • Keep the cream at a gentle simmer; a hard boil can make it split.
  • A squeeze of lemon at the table brightens the whole dish.
  • Pat sun-dried tomatoes dry before chopping so they do not slip.

Substitutions

  • Use half-and-half plus 1 teaspoon cornstarch for a lighter sauce.
  • Kale instead of spinach: add it 2 minutes earlier.
  • Gluten-free pasta works; reserve extra pasta water as it can absorb more sauce.

Variations

  • Add sliced pan-seared chicken breast.
  • Add sautéed shrimp in the final minute.
  • Fold in a handful of chopped artichoke hearts.

Storage

Refrigerate up to 3 days. Cream sauces thicken considerably when cold.

Reheating

Reheat gently on the stove with a splash of milk or stock, stirring often.

Questions

Can this be made dairy-free?

Full-fat coconut milk and a dairy-free hard cheese alternative can stand in, though the flavour will change noticeably.
