Grilled Chicken Caesar Salad

Charred chicken, cold romaine and a garlicky, anchovy-backed dressing made in a jar.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

A great Caesar depends on contrast: cold, crunchy leaves against warm chicken, a sharp dressing against rich cheese, soft lettuce against crisp croutons.

This version skips raw egg in favour of mayonnaise as the base, which makes the dressing steady and safe to keep for several days. The anchovy is not optional in spirit; it gives the depth people recognise as Caesar.

Method

  1. Shake all dressing ingredients in a jar until smooth. Taste and adjust lemon and pepper. Refrigerate.

  2. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Toss the bread with 2 tablespoons olive oil and a pinch of salt and bake 10 to 12 minutes until crisp.

  3. Butterfly or pound the chicken to an even 3/4-inch (2 cm) thickness. Rub with oil, salt and paprika.

  4. Heat a grill pan or outdoor grill to medium-high. Grill the chicken 5 to 6 minutes per side until it reaches 165°F (74°C) inside.

  5. Rest the chicken 5 minutes, then slice against the grain.

  6. Toss the romaine with about two-thirds of the dressing. Top with chicken, croutons and shaved parmesan, and pass the remaining dressing at the table.

Cook's notes

  • Pounding the chicken evenly is what keeps it from drying at the edges while the centre cooks.
  • Dry the romaine thoroughly; water dilutes the dressing and makes it slide off.
  • An instant-read thermometer removes all guesswork from grilled chicken.

Substitutions

  • Anchovy paste (about 2 teaspoons) works in place of fillets.
  • Use Greek yogurt for half the mayonnaise for a lighter, tangier dressing.
  • Chicken thighs stay juicier and grill in about the same time.

Variations

  • Add halved soft-boiled eggs and cherry tomatoes.
  • Swap chicken for grilled shrimp, cooked 2 minutes per side.
  • Make it a wrap with a large flour tortilla.

Storage

Store dressing, chicken, croutons and lettuce separately. Dressing keeps 5 days, chicken 3 days, croutons 4 days at room temperature in a sealed container.

Reheating

Serve chicken cold or warm gently in a covered skillet with a splash of water for 2 to 3 minutes.

Questions

Can I make the dressing without anchovies?

Yes. Add an extra teaspoon of Worcestershire and a little more parmesan, though the flavour will be milder. Note that Worcestershire usually contains anchovy as well.
