Strawberry Cheesecake

A smooth, dense baked cheesecake on a graham cracker crust, finished with a quick strawberry topping.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

Cheesecake asks for patience rather than skill. Room-temperature ingredients, slow mixing and a gentle oven give a smooth, crack-free top.

The strawberry topping is cooked briefly so the fruit softens but keeps some shape. It can be made a day ahead along with the cheesecake.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Wrap the outside of a 9-inch springform pan in heavy-duty foil.

  2. Mix crumbs, sugar and butter and press firmly into the base and slightly up the sides. Bake 10 minutes, then cool. Lower the oven to 325°F (165°C).

  3. Beat cream cheese, sugar and flour on low speed until completely smooth, scraping the bowl often. Mix in sour cream, vanilla and lemon juice.

  4. Add the eggs one at a time on low speed, mixing just until each disappears.

  5. Pour over the crust. Set the pan in a roasting pan and add hot water halfway up the sides. Bake 60 to 70 minutes, until the edges are set and the centre jiggles slightly.

  6. Turn off the oven, prop the door open and leave the cheesecake inside 1 hour. Then chill at least 6 hours.

  7. For the topping, simmer strawberries, sugar and lemon 5 minutes, stir in the cornstarch slurry and cook 1 minute until glossy. Cool and spoon over the cheesecake before serving.

Cook's notes

  • Cold cream cheese makes lumps that will not beat out. Take it out 2 hours ahead.
  • Low mixer speed prevents excess air, which causes cracks.
  • Cut with a hot, dry knife, wiping between slices.

Substitutions

  • Use digestive biscuits or vanilla wafers for the crust.
  • Greek yogurt can replace sour cream.
  • Frozen strawberries work for the topping; cook 2 minutes longer.

Variations

  • Blueberry or cherry topping using the same method.
  • Swirl a few spoonfuls of the cooled topping into the batter before baking.
  • Add lemon zest to the filling.

Storage

Refrigerate covered up to 5 days. Freeze the plain cheesecake, well wrapped, up to 2 months; add topping after thawing.

Reheating

Serve chilled. Thaw frozen cheesecake overnight in the refrigerator.

Questions

Why did my cheesecake crack?

Common causes are overmixing, overbaking, or cooling too quickly. The water bath and slow cool in the oven help a lot.

Do I have to use a water bath?

It is strongly recommended for an even texture. Without it, bake at 300°F (150°C) and expect a slightly firmer edge.
