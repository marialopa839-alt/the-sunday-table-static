Chocolate Lava Cake

Individual chocolate cakes with a soft, flowing centre, baked in twelve minutes.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

Lava cakes sound like restaurant desserts but are among the easiest to make at home. The batter is essentially melted chocolate, butter, eggs and a little flour, baked hot and briefly so the centre stays soft.

Because there are so few ingredients, chocolate quality matters. Use a bar you would happily eat, around 60 to 70% cacao.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Butter four 6-oz ramekins generously and dust with cocoa, tapping out the excess. Place on a baking sheet.

  2. Melt chocolate and butter together in a heatproof bowl over barely simmering water, or in short microwave bursts, stirring until smooth. Cool slightly.

  3. Whisk eggs, yolks, sugar and salt for 2 to 3 minutes until pale and slightly thickened.

  4. Fold the chocolate mixture into the eggs, then fold in the flour until just combined.

  5. Divide among ramekins and bake 11 to 13 minutes, until the edges are set but the centres still wobble.

  6. Rest 1 minute, run a knife around the edges and invert onto plates. Serve right away.

Cook's notes

  • Ovens vary. Bake one test cake first if you are serving guests.
  • Generous buttering and cocoa dusting are what let the cakes release.
  • Filled ramekins can be refrigerated up to 8 hours; add 2 to 3 minutes to the baking time.

Substitutions

  • Gluten-free: use 2 tablespoons almond flour or a gluten-free flour blend.
  • Semi-sweet chocolate gives a sweeter cake.
  • Use a muffin tin for 6 smaller cakes; bake 8 to 9 minutes.

Variations

  • Drop a square of white chocolate or a spoon of peanut butter into the centre of each before baking.
  • Add 1/2 teaspoon instant espresso to the melted chocolate.
  • Add orange zest to the batter.

Storage

Best eaten straight from the oven. Unbaked batter keeps refrigerated in the ramekins up to 1 day.

Reheating

Baked leftovers will set firm; warm 15 to 20 seconds in the microwave for a fudgy cake.

Questions

Is the centre undercooked?

The centre is intentionally soft. The eggs reach a warm temperature, but if you prefer fully cooked eggs, bake 2 to 3 minutes longer for a fudgy rather than flowing centre.
