Avocado Egg Toast

Crisp sourdough, lemony smashed avocado and a jammy egg, ready in about twelve minutes.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

Avocado toast became a punchline because it is so often made carelessly: under-seasoned avocado on soft bread. Treat each layer seriously and it is a genuinely satisfying breakfast.

The method here uses a six-and-a-half-minute egg, which gives a set white and a yolk that is thick rather than runny, so it stays on the toast. Lemon and flaky salt do most of the seasoning work.

Method

  1. Bring a small pot of water to a gentle boil. Lower the eggs in with a spoon and cook for exactly 6 1/2 minutes.

  2. Meanwhile, transfer the eggs to a bowl of cold water when the time is up and leave them for 2 minutes.

  3. Brush the bread with olive oil and toast in a skillet over medium heat or in a toaster until deeply golden.

  4. Halve the avocado, scoop it into a bowl and mash with the lemon juice and fine salt, leaving some texture.

  5. Spread the avocado thickly over the toast. Peel and halve the eggs and set them on top.

  6. Finish with flaky salt, pepper, red pepper flakes and chives. Eat right away.

Cook's notes

  • Choose an avocado that yields to gentle pressure near the stem end; firm ones will not mash smoothly.
  • Peel eggs under running water, starting at the wide end where the air pocket sits.
  • Season the avocado itself, not only the top. That is the difference between flat and bright.

Substitutions

  • Use whole-grain or gluten-free bread as preferred.
  • Lime juice works in place of lemon.
  • For an egg-free version, top with white beans dressed in olive oil.

Variations

  • Add sliced radishes and cucumber for crunch.
  • Crumble feta over the top and add a few cherry tomatoes.
  • Swap the boiled egg for a fried egg with crisp edges.

Storage

Best eaten immediately. Mashed avocado can be pressed under plastic wrap and refrigerated for up to 1 day, though it may darken slightly on top.

Reheating

Not recommended. Peeled soft-boiled eggs can be warmed in hot tap water for 1 minute.

Questions

How do I get the same yolk every time?

Use eggs straight from the refrigerator, start timing when they go into boiling water, and chill them immediately.
