Quinoa Vegetable Bowl

Roasted sweet potato and broccoli over fluffy quinoa with a bright lemon-herb dressing.

Jump to recipe

About this dish

A bowl like this is a template more than a fixed recipe. The constant is properly cooked quinoa, rinsed and simmered with a lid, then left to steam so each grain is separate.

Roasting the vegetables at high heat brings out their sweetness, and a sharp herb dressing ties everything together. It is a filling vegetarian meal with a good mix of fibre and protein.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss sweet potato, broccoli and pepper with oil and salt on a large sheet pan.

  2. Roast 25 to 30 minutes, turning once, until tender and browned at the edges.

  3. Meanwhile, combine quinoa and water in a saucepan with a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to low and simmer 15 minutes.

  4. Remove from the heat and leave covered 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

  5. Whisk or shake the dressing ingredients together.

  6. Divide quinoa and greens among bowls, top with roasted vegetables, beans and avocado, and drizzle with dressing.

Cook's notes

  • Rinse quinoa well to remove its natural bitter coating.
  • Do not crowd the pan; use two if needed so vegetables roast rather than steam.
  • Cut vegetables to similar sizes so they finish together.

Substitutions

  • Use brown rice or farro instead of quinoa.
  • Chickpeas can replace black beans.
  • Butternut squash or cauliflower can replace sweet potato or broccoli.

Variations

  • Add a fried or soft-boiled egg.
  • Top with toasted pumpkin seeds and crumbled goat cheese.
  • Swap the dressing for peanut-lime sauce.

Storage

Keeps 4 days refrigerated without the avocado and dressing. Add those fresh.

Reheating

Microwave quinoa and vegetables 1 1/2 to 2 minutes, or eat at room temperature.

Questions

Is quinoa a complete protein?

Quinoa contains all nine essential amino acids, though the amount per serving is moderate. The beans here add more protein to the bowl.
